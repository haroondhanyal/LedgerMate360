# Finance engine

The finance engine returns balanced postings for each money event. Asset increases are debits and asset decreases are credits. Income and liabilities offset asset increases; expenses and receivables offset asset decreases.

Current Phase 1 rules cover income, expense, transfers, savings transfers, loan movement, fees, interest and adjustments. Automated tests verify balancing, account effects and transfer classification. Loan amortization and receivable/payable lifecycle rules belong to Phase 2.
